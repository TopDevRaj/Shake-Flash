1.Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Shake Flashlight – Smart Torch (package name com.flashk.shake.torch, the “App”, “we”, “us”), developed by devnur, handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy. We designed the App to be private by default: it is a simple flashlight utility that works completely offline.

Key features (what the App does)

  • Turns your device's LED flash (torch) on and off instantly.
  • “Shake to Flash” — shake the phone to toggle the light, with adjustable sensitivity and shake count.
  • Pocket Protection using the proximity sensor to avoid accidental toggles.
  • Optional background mode so it works with the screen off.
  • Vibration (haptic) feedback on toggle.
  • Light / Dark / System themes, Material You dynamic colour, and premium accent colours.
  • Optional profile: add your name and a photo from your gallery (Premium).
  • Clean, professional vector (SVG) icons and animations throughout the interface.
  • Optional one-time Premium upgrade (lifetime — see section 6).

All of the above runs on-device. None of these features collect, upload, or share any personal data.

2.Information we collect

We collect NO personal data

The App does not collect, request, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts;
  • Your location (GPS, network, or otherwise);
  • Photos, camera images, video, or microphone audio;
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs;
  • Usage analytics, crash logs sent to us, or behavioural data.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to store on a server, share, or sell — we operate no servers and hold no user database.

4.Device sensors we use

The App reads two motion/position sensors purely on-device, in real time. Their readings are used only to trigger an action and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted:

  • Accelerometer — to detect a deliberate shake so the flashlight can toggle.
  • Proximity sensor — for “Pocket Protection”, to ignore shakes while the phone is covered or in a pocket.

5.Data storage & retention

Your preferences (theme, accent colour, sensitivity, shake count, and feature toggles) are saved only on your device using Android's standard local storage. This information:

  • Never leaves your phone;
  • Is not synced to any cloud or account;
  • Is automatically removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Optional profile (name & photo)

Premium users may optionally add a display name and a profile photo inside the App. If you choose to:

  • The name you type and the photo you pick are stored only on your device and are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted anywhere.
  • The photo is selected through Android's built-in Photo Picker, so the App does not request access to your gallery, media, or storage — you simply choose one image, and only that image is copied into the App's private folder.
  • You can change or remove this information at any time, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

9.Data security
